  • cachegrid · open-source Go library · in progress

    A distributed cache-compute library: memoize expensive deterministic work, shard it across nodes by consistent hashing, and replicate each shard for availability. Grew out of the live fractal service, where it shards Mandelbrot tiles across compute pods with failover. Building it in the open, with an interactive visualizer and a dev log.
