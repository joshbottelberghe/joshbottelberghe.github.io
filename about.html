About me

Who I am outside the code.

I'm a senior software engineer working on networking and distributed systems; there's more on the home page and résumé.

Away from the keyboard, I swam Division I at Notre Dame and still swim to stay in shape. A lot of the rest of my time goes to music: I've played guitar for over ten years and record at home, and I keep a drum kit around for when I need to stop thinking about a problem for a while. I cook most nights, usually on cast iron, do some yoga to stay out of my head, and read a fair amount, usually things that have nothing to do with work. For games it's mostly Factorio, Stellaris, and Rocket League. Home is my girlfriend and our two cats.
