Emoji board

A tiny guestbook, served from my desktop.

This runs on my machine and reaches the internet through the same ngrok gateway that fronts the rest of my live services — one edge, path-routed to several small Go programs. Board is a plain click tally. Chain is a mini proof-of-work chain: your browser mines a small hash, the server verifies it and links your stamp to the previous one. It's live while the desktop is on.

Click an emoji. Every click counts — that's the whole thing.
