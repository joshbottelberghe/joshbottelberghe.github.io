Fractal cache

Recompute avoidance you can watch.

A two-phase cache, modeled on the route-visualization pipeline I work on. Click to zoom, drag to pan, switch palettes to watch a recolor cost nothing.

Phase 1 · expensive Escape-time iterations per pixel, cached by (zoom, x, y, iter).
Phase 2 · cheap A color palette over those cached iterations. Changing palette recomputes nothing.
why split them Cache the expensive base once, layer the cheap part on top: meaningful work, not just faster work.
switching = phase-2 recolor, no recompute
border color = serving pod
Press compare cold vs cached to watch the cache avoid the recompute.

Compute pods — sharded & replicated

connecting…

Tiles are sharded across the pods by a consistent hash of (zoom,x,y,iter,palette), replicated 2×, so each pod owns a stable slice of the plane and a killed pod fails over to its replica. Built on cachegrid.
