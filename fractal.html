Fractal cache

Recompute avoidance you can watch.

Each 256×256 tile of this Mandelbrot view is an expensive escape-time computation on my desktop. The server keeps an LRU cache keyed by (zoom, x, y, iterations), so a tile is only ever computed once — pan or zoom back over ground you've seen and those tiles are cache hits, not recomputes. Only newly revealed tiles cost anything. It's the same idea behind the route-visualization pipeline I work on: do less work, not faster work. Click to zoom in; drag to pan.
