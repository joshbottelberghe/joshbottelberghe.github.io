Josh Bottelberghe

Software engineer working on networking, distributed systems, and infrastructure at scale.

Hi, I'm Josh. I'm an engineer in Vancouver, Washington, where I live with my girlfriend and our two cats. Before I wrote software I swam competitively, Division I at Notre Dame, and a lot of how I work still traces back to that: show up consistently, measure honestly, and trust that steady reps beat heroics. Away from the keyboard I play guitar and drums and record at home, cook most nights on cast iron, and lose the occasional weekend to Factorio.

I like building things that are simple, well-instrumented, and genuinely useful to the people who depend on them. The rest of this site is the work itself: what I've built, how I think about it, and writeups that go a level deeper.

Day to day I build the control- and data-plane systems that keep large networks legible: taking live routing state off a fleet of nodes and turning it into something operators and customers can actually reason about. That means Go, BGP/FRR internals, and the usual at-scale toolkit like Kubernetes, AWS, Redis, and S3. I'm most drawn to the seam where networking meets distributed systems: keeping a pipeline fast and correct when the cost of doing the obvious thing grows with the size of the world instead of the size of the change.
