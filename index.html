Josh Bottelberghe

Senior software engineer · networking, distributed systems, and infrastructure at scale.

I am Josh Bottelberghe, a Senior Software Engineer at Alkira, Inc. I work on the routing/control-plane team, delivering routing services to meet customer needs for global network visibility and routing control (policy, segmentation, NAT, and more). While my work in the fast-paced startup environment takes me across the stack with new, unique, and difficult problems every day, my main focus has been my ownership of the route-visualization services, which give route views at various levels within a customer's complicated, multi-cloud, segmented network. Across the routing services suite I work closely with my team in code review, building on and contributing to each other's designs. On a small team that means everyone has to stay aware of what's going on and be actively engaged with the team's work, to ensure nothing slips and the customer's concerns are addressed promptly and accurately.

When I'm not working, I spend my time swimming, playing guitar, cooking, and with my girlfriend and our two cats. My time swimming Division I at Notre Dame and the fast-paced nature of a startup have taught me the necessity of responsibility and the meaning of accountability, not just within engineering, but in life and to each other.

This site is a work in progress. A few sections still have gaps I'm filling in, and some of the writing is AI-drafted for now until I get to it.
