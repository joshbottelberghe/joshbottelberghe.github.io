Josh Bottelberghe

Senior software engineer · networking, distributed systems, and infrastructure at scale.

Senior Software Engineer at Alkira, on the routing / control-plane team. I build routing services that give customers visibility and control over complex multi-cloud networks (policy, segmentation, NAT). The largest piece of that is the route-visualization system, which I own end to end, from the compute pipeline to the customer-facing APIs. The rest of the job is the stack around it: design work on our other routing services, node-side systems, production debugging across the team's services, CI and dependency security for the whole repo fleet, and the tooling the team runs on.

Outside work: swimming, guitar, cooking, and time with my girlfriend and our two cats. I swam Division I at Notre Dame.
