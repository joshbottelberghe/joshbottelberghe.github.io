Josh Bottelberghe

Software engineer working on networking, distributed systems, and infrastructure at scale.

Hi, I'm Josh. I'm a software engineer in Vancouver, Washington, and I live with my girlfriend and our two cats. Before I got into software I was a Division I swimmer at Notre Dame, and a lot of how I work still comes from that: showing up every day and doing the work, even the boring parts. Outside of work I play guitar and drums and record at home, cook most nights on cast iron, and put too many hours into Factorio.

I care about building things that are simple, hold up under load, and are actually useful to the people relying on them. A lot of the time that means cutting the work instead of doing it faster. The rest of this site is the actual work: what I've built, how I think about it, and a few writeups that go deeper.

Most of my work is on network control-plane systems: the ones that take the live routing state off a fleet of nodes and turn it into something operators and customers can actually reason about. Day to day that's Go, BGP and FRR internals, and the usual at-scale stuff, Kubernetes, AWS, Redis, S3. The part I like most is where networking meets distributed systems, keeping a pipeline fast and correct when the naive approach costs you more as the whole system grows, not just as the changes do.
